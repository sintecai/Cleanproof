# Privacy and service policy — internal working draft

Not approved customer terms or a compliance certification. Sicy International Ltd must supply its registered address, privacy/support contact and approved controller/processor responsibilities before launch. Review actual implementation and legal duties, including customer processing agreements where needed.

CleanProof is intended to process business/account contacts, team permissions, customer/site details, inspection answers/notes/photos, corrective actions, history and reports. Minimise identifiable people, client documents, access codes and unrelated sensitive material. Choose and document purposes/lawful bases, regions/subprocessors, retention, transfer safeguards, rights/complaint procedures, incident handling and backup expiry. Do not invent a retention promise.

The present private preview uses synthetic records and local encrypted browser storage. Browser clearing/eviction can remove local data. Native SQLCipher/device-key protection still needs device acceptance. Configured backend flows use Supabase Auth and private data/media services, but actual-service acceptance is pending. No password, token, verification code, photo or inspection note belongs in logs or support messages.

Local PDF exports are ordinary files and cannot be recalled. Issued report links, company/account self-service deletion/export, team invitation management, manager role and subscription entitlements are not yet delivered. Do not advertise them as working. Define support, availability, customer responsibilities, termination and liability only after management/legal review. No production SLA, certification, guaranteed restore or paid subscription is offered by this draft.

A future controlled free pilot needs explicit server grants, agreed limits/duration/support, privacy/contract approval, tested export/deletion and passed customer-data release gates. Policy publication alone does not confer readiness.
