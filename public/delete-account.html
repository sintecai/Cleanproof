Delete your CleanProof account

CleanProof is provided by Sicy International Ltd. Account deletion requires authentication so that another person cannot remove your account merely by knowing your email address.

  1. Open CleanProof and sign in to the account you want to delete. If necessary, use Forgot password first.
  2. Open Settings → Delete my account. Sign out and sign in again immediately before deletion.
  3. Read the company and saved-work information. If other active team members depend on you as their only owner, transfer ownership before proceeding.
  4. Choose whether to close companies where you are the only active member, type the confirmation phrase, then confirm deletion.

Your login, company access and personal onboarding records are removed. Closing an eligible sole-member company also removes its server records and private photographs. Shared company inspection histories remain with the company's other owner. Downloaded PDF copies cannot be recalled. Device-only encrypted work and unsent queues are not erased by server account deletion and cannot synchronise after deletion; export valuable records first.

Backup retention, production support details and the final public URL require approval before store submission. This page is not a promise that the current staging service has completed deletion.
