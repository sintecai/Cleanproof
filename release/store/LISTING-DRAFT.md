## Full description (both stores)
CleanProof helps commercial cleaning teams record site inspections and keep clear evidence of their work.

Organise customers and sites, build versioned checklists, record Pass, Fail or Not Applicable answers, add comments and photographs, and track corrective actions. Reopen saved inspections and prepare branded reports for your records.

Saved-on-device work and synchronisation status are shown separately. An internet connection is required for account services and server synchronisation. Reports exported to your device are separate copies and cannot be recalled by revoking service access.

Provided by Sicy International Ltd.
